ROOT CAUSE: Compromised VPN credentials (no MFA enforced on SSL-VPN).
CVE-2023-27997 (FortiOS heap overflow) allowed authentication bypass.

LESSONS LEARNED:
1. MFA must be enforced on ALL remote access points
2. Service accounts should not have Domain Admin privileges
3. EDR tampering alerts must be treated as P1
4. Offline/immutable backups are essential
5. Network segmentation would have limited lateral movement
